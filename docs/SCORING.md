# Deterministic Scoring Methodology

CanadaOpportunityGraph rejects opaque, non-deterministic LLM score generation. All numerical scores (0–100) are computed via strictly deterministic algorithms implemented in `internal/scoring/engine.go` and `internal/sovereignty/engine.go`.

---

## 1. Buildability Score (`buildability-v1.0`)
**Question Answered:** *How likely is this project to reach operational execution?*

### Weighting Breakdown:
1. **Stage Progression Maturity (15%)**: Numerical progression from `DISCOVERED` (10 pts) up to `FID` / `CONSTRUCTION` (95 pts) and `OPERATING` (100 pts).
2. **Financing Readiness (15%)**: Ratio of closed/committed capital against reported CAPEX ($ CAD).
3. **Indigenous Agreements (15%)**: Presence of formal First Nations/Inuit/Métis Impact Benefit Agreements (IBAs) or equity joint ventures.
4. **Regulatory & Environmental Approvals (15%)**: Key milestones achieved under the Impact Assessment Act or Canadian Energy Regulator.
5. **Land & Site Control (10%)**: Confirmed site acquisition, legal title, and surveyed geographic boundaries.
6. **Commercial Offtake & PPAs (10%)**: Long-term bilateral power purchase or commodity purchase agreements.
7. **Infrastructure & Grid Interconnect Readiness (10%)**: Substation and transmission line capacity confirmation.
8. **Proponent Credibility (10%)**: Proponent institutional track record (e.g. major Crown utility vs junior exploratory venture).

## 2. Investability Score (`investability-v1.0`)
**Question Answered:** *Is there an attractive, actionable capital opportunity around this asset?*

### Weighting Breakdown:
- **Capital Scale & Gap (25%)**: Dollar magnitude of total CAPEX.
- **Financing Velocity (25%)**: Rate of recent commitments and presence of Crown co-investment (CIB, CGF).
- **Strategic Importance (25%)**: Critical minerals, baseload clean power, or Arctic sovereignty mandate.
- **Execution Feasibility (25%)**: Downside risk protection and milestone certainty.

---

## 3. Supplierability Score (`supplierability-v1.0`)
**Question Answered:** *How strong is the opportunity for contractors, engineers, and suppliers?*

### Weighting Breakdown:
- **Procurement Proximity (30%)**: Lifecycle proximity to active tendering.
- **Tender Pipeline Volume (25%)**: Active notices published on CanadaBuys.
- **Technical Intensity (25%)**: Nuclear, specialized mining, or high-voltage engineering density.
- **Downstream Opportunities (20%)**: Volume of derived requirements generated by the propagation engine.

---

## 4. Canadian AI Sovereignty Index (`cai-sovereignty-v1.0`)
- **Data Residency (15%)**: 100% data at rest stored within Canadian borders.
- **Compute Residency (15%)**: GPUs physically operating in Canadian facilities.
- **Canadian Ownership (15%)**: Domestic equity control and governance.
- **Foreign Extraterritorial Legal Exposure (15%)**: Immunity from US CLOUD Act Section 103 and FISA Section 702.
- **Deployment Autonomy (15%)**: Local and air-gapped disconnected execution capability.
- **Bilingual Parity (10%)**: Equal-quality Canadian English and French model capability.
- **Quebec Law 25 Compliance (10%)**: DPIA, strict consent, and biometric controls.
- **Clean Energy Provenance (5%)**: Hydro / nuclear power share of data centre cluster.

# CanadaOpportunityGraph Methodology

This document outlines the theoretical and empirical methodology underpinning the graph structure, scoring models, and opportunity propagation algorithms.

---

## 1. Graph Data Model & Ontological Foundations

Economic buildout occurs through interdependent physical and institutional events. CanadaOpportunityGraph represents this as a directed, multi-relational temporal graph:

* **Nodes**:
  - `Project`: Physical capital expenditure asset located in Canadian geographic space.
  - `Organization`: Proponent, contractor, financier, First Nation, or regulatory body.
  - `Opportunity`: Downstream requirement generated by upstream project milestones.
  - `Procurement`: Formal public or private contracting vehicle.
  - `Evidence`: Primary statutory document, filing, or certified claim.
* **Edges**:
  - `PROPONENT_OF`, `CONTRACTOR_FOR`, `FINANCED_BY`, `REGULATED_BY`, `SUPPLIES_TO`, `GENERATES_OPPORTUNITY`.

---

## 2. Downstream Opportunity Propagation Engine

When an upstream project advances its development stage, physical infrastructure realities necessitate downstream goods and services. The propagation engine (`internal/propagation/engine.go`) maps these linkages deterministically:

1. **Rule Definitions**: Sectoral dependency rules (e.g. `NUCLEAR_SMR` -> `TURBINE_ISLAND_EPC`, `GRID_INTERCONNECT_HV`, `RAD_WASTE_CONTAINMENT`).
2. **Confidence Classification**:
   - `CONFIRMED`: Linked directly to an awarded contract or active public tender.
   - `DERIVED`: Inferred from mandatory engineering specifications associated with project scale (e.g., >100MW requires dedicated substation).
   - `SPECULATIVE`: Early-stage anticipated requirements subject to final investment decision (FID).
3. **Timing Windows**: Estimated delivery lead times (e.g. 18–36 months prior to commercial operation date).

---

## 3. Canadian Capital Stack Intelligence

The platform models Canadian public-private capital layering (`internal/capitalstack/engine.go`):

* **Clean Tech ITC**: 30% refundable tax credit under Section 127.45 of the Income Tax Act.
* **Clean Electricity ITC**: 15% refundable credit for non-taxable entities (Crown corps, Indigenous corps).
* **Clean Technology Manufacturing ITC (CMITC)**: 30% for extraction, processing, and recycling of critical minerals.
* **Canada Infrastructure Bank (CIB)**: Concessionary loans, commercial debt, and equity participation.
* **Canada Growth Fund (CGF)**: Carbon contracts for difference (CCfDs) and cleantech scale-up equity.
* **Strategic Innovation Fund (SIF)**: Net Zero Accelerator capital contributions.

The engine verifies program eligibility rules and highlights stacking constraints (e.g., prohibition of claiming both Clean Tech ITC and Clean Electricity ITC on the exact same property).
